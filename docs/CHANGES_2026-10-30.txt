2. JOINT vs SEQUENTIAL ALNS (new: traffic/alns.py, traffic/run_alns.py, traffic/test_alns.py)
- Both arms run the same ALNS search: same start, operators, seed and time budget.
  Only the score differs:
    sequential = traffic-free A* makespan; prioritized planning routes the winner afterwards
    joint      = prioritized-planning makespan inside the search
- Plans are made offline before each run. ALNS does not re-plan during a run.
- Workload: 24 workstations (the 12 DOE bays plus 12 more), 6 robots, capacity 2
  (parts must be redeployed), any robot may pick up any READY bay.
- Drop rule: the robot turns, waits the 2 s DWELL, and leaves. The workstation then
  processes for 30 s and becomes READY. The robot does not wait for processing.
- Frozen experiment plans: generated_plans/ALNS24_joint.json and ALNS24_seq.json.
- Run order for the 5 pairs: J/S, S/J, S/J, J/S, S/J.
- November 2, 2026, before any experiment run: extended to 8 pairs.
  Order: J/S, S/J, S/J, J/S, S/J, J/S, S/J, J/S (4 joint-first, 4 sequential-first).
  If time runs out, stop after a complete pair; the stopping point is not chosen from results.


3. SUPERVISOR FIXES (fleet/fleetSupervisor.py, fleet/dispatch_advisor.py, fleet/camera_grid_navigate.py)
Deadlocks seen in today's shakedowns, each fixed and covered by a test:
- Lane head-on: two robots on neighbouring bay entries both heading into the lattice
  node between them. A robot may no longer move onto an entry while another robot on
  the far entry needs that node. (test_lane_head_on.py)
- Leaving a workstation: a robot in a workstation now also waits while another robot's
  next move is onto a node beside its entry, not only while one is standing there.
  (test_exit_approach_trim.py)
- Small heading trims: a robot going straight through a node may skip a blocked trim
  of 10 deg or less when the exact footprints stay at least 0.25 in apart.
  (test_exit_approach_trim.py)
- Step-aside choice: the deadlock resolver looked 2 nodes ahead instead of 3 (it counted
  plan items, and turn items sit between moves), so it parked robots inside the other
  robot's turn. It now compares every spot within one or two moves against both robots'
  next nodes and other robots' routes. (test_yield_choice.py)
- No room to turn: a robot that cannot turn may leave straight ahead (along the lane or
  into its own workstation). As a last resort it may back straight out with negative
  wheel speeds; no firmware change was needed. (test_yield_choice.py, test_reverse_yield.py)
  Reversing has not yet happened on the robots.
- Switching between 4 and 6 robots no longer stalls the supervisor. A status message from
  a robot outside the current mission raised an error that ended the ROS spin thread.
  The supervisor now runs run after run without a restart. (test_mission_switch.py)
- Drop rule above implemented in the supervisor's pick-up gate. (test_pickup_gate.py)

November 3, 2026, 15:46 -- Alvik2 waited forever in bay 42 (joint, pair 1 rerun)

  registered.

  dispatch of the exit. Alvik3's exit from bay 27 failed and was retried,
